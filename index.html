About

From lighting Marvel and Final Fantasy shots to building the AI agents I always wanted to see.

I started as a CG artist, not an engineer. BFA in Illustration from the University of the Arts in Philadelphia, then out to LA for Gnomon School of Visual Effects and early Hollywood work, then nine years in Tokyo, immersed in VR, CG, and anime projects, including two and a half years at Square Enix building pipeline tools and lighting shots for Final Fantasy and Kingdom Hearts (I did the key art for Final Fantasy VII Remake). Lighting on Marvel's Thor: Love and Thunder at Method Studios came after. Writing the tools other artists relied on is what pulled me into software full time. Today that's full-stack engineering across backend, frontend, mobile, gaming, and VR: agentic AI platforms, spatial and volumetric video tooling, VR games built from the ground up, and enterprise 3D commerce used by major retailers.

Outside of code, I play sax, flute, and jazz piano. Feel free to explore the projects above, and get in touch if you want to talk about a role or a build.
